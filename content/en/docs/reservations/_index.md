# Reservations

[Reservations](https://podstack.ai/app/reservations) are dedicated clusters of 16 to 1,024 GPUs for a fixed term, without preemption.

Choose a GPU, a cluster size, and a term, then request a quote. Podstack replies within one business day. There is no self-serve price list for reservations in these docs. The quote is the price.
