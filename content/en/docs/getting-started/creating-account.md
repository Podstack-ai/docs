# Create an account

Open [podstack.ai/auth](https://podstack.ai/auth) and choose one of:

- Continue with Google
- Continue with Microsoft
- Continue with GitHub

The first time you sign in, Podstack creates your account and a personal project. You land in the console at `/app`.

There is no separate signup form and no identity-verification step before you can launch.

An **Administrator sign-in** control is on the same page. That is not how customer accounts are created.

By continuing you agree to the [terms](https://podstack.ai/terms) and the acceptable use policy.
