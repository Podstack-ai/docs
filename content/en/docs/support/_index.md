# Support

Help for everything around running on Podstack — getting into your account, understanding a charge, finishing KYC, and fixing technical issues. Most questions are answered by the guides here; for anything account-, billing-, or incident-specific, contact support.

## Getting help fast

- **Email [support@podstack.ai](mailto:support@podstack.ai)** — the primary channel, target reply within 24 hours. Billing questions can also go to [billing@podstack.ai](mailto:billing@podstack.ai).
- **Contact Us page** — a contact form and the support phone line (**+91 96205 98447**, Mon-Fri 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM IST) at [dashboard.podstack.ai/contact-us](https://dashboard.podstack.ai/contact-us).
- **Compute Advisor** — the in-portal AI assistant (floating button on the dashboard) that recommends the right GPU for your workload. It's for choosing a GPU, not a support agent.
- **These docs** — [Troubleshooting](/docs/support/troubleshooting/), [Scenarios](/docs/support/scenarios/), and [FAQs](/docs/support/faqs/).

Full details on every channel and what to include in a request: [Getting Help](/docs/support/getting-help/).

## When to use support

Reach for support when you need to:

- **Get into your account** — sign-in codes, lost access, sessions (Podstack is passwordless).
- **Understand or dispute billing** — a charge, an invoice, run rate, or a refund.
- **Finish KYC** — a verification that's stuck or was rejected.
- **Get GPU access** — a GPU type or region that isn't currently available.
- **Fix a technical issue** — pods, storage, SSH, networking, or the CLI.
